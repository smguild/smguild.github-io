002
Envoy Airlines · Summer 2025 Educational Design Capstone Training Redesign

Flight Attendant Training Redesign

Redesign of a 130-slide, four-hour training curriculum for new hire flight attendants at Envoy Airlines. Led content strategy, instructional design, and technical writing to improve engagement and comprehension—aligning materials with FAA standards and shaping how crew understand their roles from day one.

D1 — Customer Preboarding Before / After
Before: Photo-only slide with no on-screen text to anchor key points.
After: Smooth transitions and minimal supporting text added to guide students through content.
D2 — Originating Flights Before / After
Before: All concepts on one slide, causing information overload.
After: Each concept revealed one-by-one, directing student attention sequentially.
D3 — Prior to Takeoff Compliance Check Before / After
Before: No IPM citation, students unable to quickly locate the relevant manual section.
After: IPM chapter, section number, and title added directly to the slide.
